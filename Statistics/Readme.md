## Statistics

**Topic:** Mean, median, and mode

**What I learned:**
- **Mean** is the average. Add all values, then divide by how many there are.
- **Median** is the middle value when the data is sorted.
- **Mode** is the value that appears most often.
- The mean is affected by very large or very small values (outliers). The median is not.

**Example:**
Data: 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10, 40

- Mean = (2 + 3 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 10 + 40) / 7 = 70 / 7 = **10**
- Median = **5** (the middle value)
- Mode = **3** (appears twice)

**What this shows:**
The value 40 pulls the mean up to 10. But most values are small. So here the median (5) gives a better picture of a "typical" value.

**Mistake I made:**
I found the median without sorting the data first. The median only works on sorted data.
